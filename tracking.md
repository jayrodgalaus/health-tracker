### Example weekly rhythm (suggested, not yet logged)

- **Monday / Wednesday / Friday:** Full-body strength — pull-ups, push-ups, squats, lateral raises; optional glute bridges for posterior-chain balance.
- **Tuesday / Saturday:** Walks or other comfortable conditioning; try brisk intervals if desired.
- **Thursday / Sunday:** Rest or easy movement.
- **Effort:** Most sets can stop 1–2 technically clean reps before failure; occasional last-set technical failure is fine. Rest roughly 2–3 minutes between demanding compound sets.
- **Progression:** Aim for one additional clean rep somewhere when performance and recovery permit. Quality of technique matters more than soreness or hitting a deadline.
- **Support:** Eat adequately, include regular protein-rich foods, sleep consistently, and avoid escalating training volume too aggressively.
